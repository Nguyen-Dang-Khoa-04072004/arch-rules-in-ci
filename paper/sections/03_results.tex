\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

The analyses use 4,321 repository--tool rows from 4,300 repositories, 11,098 rule files, and 86,339 edits.
H1.1 holds for import-linter and for dependency-cruiser.
H2.1 holds for import-linter only.
The 24-month Kaplan--Meier estimate does not support H3.1.
H3.2 holds.
Section~\ref{sec:rq4} reports violation trajectories around ArchUnit adoption.

\subsection{RQ1: CI Integration}
\label{sec:rq1}

Table~\ref{tab:ci_results} gives the share of rows whose GitHub Actions workflows meet the tool-specific test.
Import-linter is invoked in 351 of 826 rows (42.5\%; 95\% CI 39.4--45.8\%).
Dependency-cruiser is invoked in 125 of 1,073 rows (11.6\%; 95\% CI 9.8--13.5\%).
Both intervals lie entirely below 50\%, so H1.1 holds for each tool.
ArchUnit has no dedicated workflow command.
A Maven or Gradle build-or-test proxy is present in 869 of 2,421 rows with a workflow result (35.9\%; 95\% CI 34.1--37.9\%).
That proxy is outside H1.1.

\subsection{RQ2: Parsed Constructs}
\label{sec:rq2}

Classification uses the 21,713 held-out constructs.
Table~\ref{tab:rule_taxonomy} lists the seven mapped categories.
The 2,901 uncategorized constructs are omitted from the proportions that follow.
Dependency and layering account for 98.1\% of parsed import-linter constructs (95\% CI 97.5--98.6\%), 58.5\% of parsed ArchUnit constructs (57.8--59.3\%), and 56.8\% of parsed dependency-cruiser constructs (54.4--59.1\%).
Only the import-linter interval lies entirely above 70\%, so H2.1 holds for import-linter only.
Among parsed dependency-cruiser constructs, cycle constraints are 649 of 1,503 (43.2\%).
Naming and annotation are 18.5\% and 8.4\% of parsed ArchUnit constructs.
The category mix differs by tool ($\chi^{2} = 5865.56$, $df = 12$, Cram\'er's $V = 0.395$).

\subsection{RQ3: Rule Rot}
\label{sec:rq3}

Rule rot is the first exempt or disable edit of a rule file.
The Kaplan--Meier incidence at 24 months is 26.3\% (95\% CI 25.4--27.2\%), below the 30\% threshold in H3.1.
The complete-case share is 49.3\% among 5,122 files (95\% CI 48.0--50.7\%) and is separate from that test.
By tool, the 24-month incidence is 12.9\% for ArchUnit (8,579 files; 95\% CI 12.1--13.8\%), 58.1\% for import-linter (1,093 files; 54.0--62.3\%), and 80.5\% for dependency-cruiser (1,426 files; 78.4--82.7\%).

After the introducing commit, 5,501 of 11,098 rule files (49.6\%; 95\% CI 48.6--50.4\%) are never modified.
The share is below the 73.8\% reported for committed AI-configuration artifacts~\cite{denisov2026ase}, so H3.2 holds.
The never-modified share is 55.7\% for ArchUnit (95\% CI 54.6--56.6\%), 13.9\% for import-linter (12.1--15.9\%), and 40.2\% for dependency-cruiser (37.7--42.7\%).

Restructure is the residual class and covers 69,047 of the 86,339 edits (80.0\%).
The other classes are add (8,448; 9.8\%), loosen (3,534; 4.1\%), exempt (3,219; 3.7\%), tighten (1,187; 1.4\%), and disable (904; 1.0\%).
A Cox model for time to the first loosen or exempt edit gives a hazard ratio of 1.307 (95\% CI 1.270--1.345) for the log file-level commit count and 0.850 (0.793--0.911) for the log author count.
The Kaplan--Meier incidence of that event at 24 months is 41.9\% (95\% CI 40.9--43.0\%).

\subsection{RQ4: Adoption Trajectories}
\label{sec:rq4}

Adoption is the first commit that introduces a parseable ArchUnit rule.
Of the 2,422 ArchUnit rows, 317 have at least 12 months of history on each side of that commit.
The replay evaluates dependency and layering constraints only.
Mean coverage of that family is 52.9\%, and mean evaluable coverage is 33.5\%.
Among 298 repositories observed one month before adoption and at adoption, 240 (80.5\%) are ratifiers and 7 (2.3\%) are reformers.

Counts at one month before adoption, at adoption, and 12 months afterward are available for 141 of the 148 repositories with at least one translated rule.
The median count is 0 at each point.
The means are 73.3 (95\% CI 23.9--138.2), 85.6 (28.6--154.8), and 103.9 (33.8--196.2).
From the first of these points to the last, 18 series decrease (12.8\%; 95\% CI 7.1--17.7\%), 86 are unchanged (61.0\%; 53.2--69.5\%), and 37 increase (26.2\%; 19.1--33.3\%).
Within-repository ordinary least squares gives a mean slope of 1.63 violations per month in the pre-adoption year (95\% CI $-0.09$ to 4.38) and 1.83 in the following year (0.47 to 3.74).
The series describe repositories that already adopted ArchUnit.
